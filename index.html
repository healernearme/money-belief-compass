The money belief system you didn't choose

Most of what shapes how we handle money isn't really about money. It's about safety, worth, belonging, and what we picked up, often long before we had words for it, about wanting things for ourselves.

This is a mix of statements to agree or disagree with, some quick scenarios, a few memory prompts, and one round where you'll pick from a table of thirty words. Together they map a specific, personal set of beliefs that may be quietly shaping your relationship with money. Not a generic "money type", your own particular pattern.

Where these connect

This tool reflects patterns in how you answered a couple of dozen prompts and one bigger word round, on one particular day. It's a starting point for curiosity, not a fixed label or a clinical assessment. Beliefs like these usually formed for a good reason, as ways of staying safe. Naming them is often the first step in loosening their grip, and it's rarely something to do entirely alone if it brings up a lot.
